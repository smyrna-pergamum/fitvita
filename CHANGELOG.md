# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)
- GPS: also uses the platform fused/network providers, accepts approximate permission, asks for
  location at start and offers to switch location on.
- Internet radio tab (Radio Browser), meditation music with sleep timer, guided workouts with
  countdowns, weight/BMI/goal tracking, four themes with male/female character; history moved to Settings.
- Colourful gamified redesign: Activity home with walking buddy, daily quests, XP, levels, streaks,
  badges and confetti; water tracker; yoga poses and flows; guided breathing; gamified reminders.
- Light/dark/system theme; 7 languages (tr, en, de, es, fr, ru, ar).
- Simple workout logging with ready and own routines, rest timer.
- 22 illustrated exercises (own artwork, animated start/end pose).
- GPS recording of walks, hikes, jogs and runs on OpenStreetMap; share/save as picture, GPX export.
- Step counter with daily goal, walked distance and 7-day bars; steps per GPS activity.
- Combined history, encrypted backup and restore, Turkish and English.
