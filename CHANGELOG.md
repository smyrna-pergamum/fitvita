# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Workout tab: "Start your own workout", stopwatch and countdown timer, new routines (glutes, legs,
  waist & belly toning), yoga and pilates flows and exercises moved here.
- 16 new illustrated exercises, including 6 pilates exercises and two new pilates flows.
- "Inner peace" tab (was Yoga): 4 meditation routines with nature sounds, box and 4-7-8 breathing,
  body scan, grounding, muscle relaxation and gratitude, plus nature sounds and meditation music.
- Player: next button for music, nature sounds and radio (also in the notification); the mini
  player now shows on the Radio tab too.
- Fix: a tab no longer reopens a workout screen started from it; tabs always open at their start.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-30)
- Nature sounds: 10 endlessly looping ambient sounds with pictures, next to the meditation music.
- Pilates: 6 illustrated exercises and a "Pilates basics" flow on the Yoga & Pilates tab.
- 8 new beginner badges; every badge shows its condition, tap for details and progress.
- Themes renamed Blue / Dark / Pink / Brown; Brown (dark brown, female character) replaces Light.
- Radio: "All" filter.
- Weight & BMI: female/male choice with a reference weight (Devine formula).
- Fix: after a workout or yoga flow, "Back to Activity" returns to the home screen.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-29)
- GPS: also uses the platform fused/network providers, accepts approximate permission, asks for
  location at start and offers to switch location on.
- Internet radio tab (Radio Browser), meditation music with sleep timer, guided workouts with
  countdowns, weight/BMI/goal tracking, four themes with male/female character; history moved to Settings.
- Colourful gamified redesign: Activity home with walking buddy, daily quests, XP, levels, streaks,
  badges and confetti; water tracker; yoga poses and flows; guided breathing; gamified reminders.
- Light/dark/system theme; 7 languages (tr, en, de, es, fr, ru, ar).
- Simple workout logging with ready and own routines, rest timer.
- 22 illustrated exercises (own artwork, animated start/end pose).
- GPS recording of walks, hikes, jogs and runs on OpenStreetMap; share/save as picture, GPX export.
- Step counter with daily goal, walked distance and 7-day bars; steps per GPS activity.
- Combined history, encrypted backup and restore, Turkish and English.
